## Mode

Operate

## Protected structure

- Main navigation keeps 用户画像、地域对比、状态对比、核心洞察、区域特征。
- These five analysis modules retain their information architecture, module structure, and core logic.
- Data Center is intentionally restructured into 数据集 and 数据集设置.

## Data Center contract

- Flow: upload or select a dataset, then configure that dataset.
- Dataset settings combine field management, persona fields, state variables, geography, time, AI derivation, and AI ordering.
- Cleaning uses an internal standard. There is no custom cleaning-rule management surface.

## Visual contract

- `#F5F5F7` canvas, white surfaces, `#1D1D1F` text, `#86868B` meta, `#007AFF` accent.
- Title + Controls + Data; no promotional secondary copy.
- 8pt spacing, 12px controls, 16px independent objects, 24px overlays.
- No gradients, dark sidebar, purple AI theme, decorative color, or nested cards.
